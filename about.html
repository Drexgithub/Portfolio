A bit about me

Hi, I'm Kiel, a web developer who believes in the power of simplicity. I build digital experiences that are clean, intuitive, and focused on what matters most: the people who use them.

With a strong foundation in modern web technologies, I enjoy turning complex problems into elegant solutions. I pay close attention to the small details (the spacing, the typography, the subtle interactions), because they're what make a good experience feel great.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me exploring new technologies, contributing to open-source, or looking for inspiration in design, architecture, and everyday objects.

My Philosophy

"Good design is as little design as possible. Less, but better, because it concentrates on the essential aspects, and the products are not burdened with non-essentials."
